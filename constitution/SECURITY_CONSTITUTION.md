# SECURITY CONSTITUTION

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Chapter I

## Security Philosophy

### Article 1 — Purpose

Security exists to protect learners, educators, institutions, educational data, digital identities, and the integrity of the Freumia ecosystem.

Security shall be considered a constitutional responsibility rather than an optional technical feature.

---

### Article 2 — Security by Design

Every Freumia system shall be designed with security as a foundational principle.

Security shall be integrated into architecture, engineering, identity, artificial intelligence, infrastructure, and operations from the beginning of development.

---

### Article 3 — Privacy by Default

Personal information shall only be collected when necessary for legitimate educational purposes.

Every user shall receive reasonable privacy protections regardless of account type.

Privacy shall remain a constitutional right within the Freumia ecosystem.

---

### Article 4 — Identity Protection

Digital identities issued by Freumia, including educational identity numbers, shall be protected against unauthorized access, misuse, impersonation, and fraudulent activity.

Identity verification shall remain an essential component of protected educational services.

---

### Article 5 — Access Control

Access to educational services shall follow the principle of least privilege.

Users shall receive only the permissions necessary for their verified constitutional role.

Authentication alone shall never grant privileged educational access.

---

### Article 6 — Data Protection

Educational information shall be securely stored, transmitted, and processed.

Freumia shall continuously improve safeguards that protect educational records from unauthorized disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

---

### Article 7 — Secure Engineering

Security reviews shall be considered during the design, implementation, testing, and maintenance of major systems.

Critical vulnerabilities should be addressed responsibly and without unnecessary delay.

---

### Article 8 — Responsible Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence shall respect constitutional security and privacy principles.

AI systems shall never intentionally expose confidential educational information or violate user trust.

---

### Article 9 — Transparency

When significant security issues affect users, Freumia shall communicate responsibly while protecting the safety of the ecosystem and its community.

Transparency shall strengthen trust without unnecessarily increasing risk.

---

### Article 10 — Continuous Security

Security is a continuous responsibility.

Freumia shall regularly improve its security practices as technology, threats, and educational environments evolve.

---

# Chapter II

## Security Principles

Freumia Security shall always prioritize:

- Protection before convenience
- Privacy before unnecessary data collection
- Verification before privileged access
- Security before rapid deployment
- Prevention before recovery
- Integrity before modification
- Trust before complexity
- Continuous improvement before complacency

---

# Chapter III

## Constitutional Security Rule

Security protects the future of Freumia.

Every architectural decision, engineering implementation, artificial intelligence capability, identity system, partnership, infrastructure service, and educational platform shall preserve the confidentiality, integrity, availability, and trustworthiness of the Freumia ecosystem.

No feature shall intentionally weaken constitutional security principles.
