# AI CONSTITUTION

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Chapter I

## Artificial Intelligence Philosophy

### Article 1 — Purpose of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence exists within Freumia to assist human learning, expand educational opportunity, and improve understanding.

AI shall never become a substitute for human curiosity, critical thinking, creativity, or independent learning.

---

### Article 2 — Human-Centered AI

Freumia shall always place learners before algorithms.

Artificial Intelligence shall serve people rather than requiring people to adapt to technology.

Educational outcomes shall remain the highest priority.

---

### Article 3 — AI Independence

Freumia shall remain independent of any single AI provider.

The AI architecture shall support multiple providers and future technologies without requiring the ecosystem to be rebuilt.

No individual AI model shall become a permanent dependency.

---

### Article 4 — Responsible AI

Artificial Intelligence shall operate responsibly.

AI responses should promote learning, understanding, honesty, and educational growth.

The platform shall avoid encouraging misinformation, academic dishonesty, manipulation, or harmful behavior.

---

### Article 5 — AI Transparency

Where appropriate, learners should understand that AI provides educational assistance.

AI-generated content should support learning rather than replace genuine educational effort.

---

### Article 6 — Personalization

Artificial Intelligence may personalize learning based on verified educational information.

Personalization shall always respect privacy, fairness, and the learner's best interests.

---

### Article 7 — Educational Integrity

AI shall encourage learners to think, solve problems, and develop understanding.

Freumia shall not intentionally design AI systems that discourage independent learning.

---

### Article 8 — Privacy

Artificial Intelligence shall process educational information responsibly.

Student data shall never be used beyond authorized educational purposes.

Privacy protections shall remain consistent with the Security Constitution.

---

### Article 9 — Continuous Improvement

The AI layer shall continuously evolve as technology advances.

Improvements shall preserve constitutional principles while enabling innovation.

---

### Article 10 — Future Readiness

Freumia's AI architecture shall remain adaptable to future models, research, and educational technologies.

Future innovation shall strengthen the ecosystem without compromising trust or educational quality.

---

# Chapter II

## AI Engineering Principles

Freumia AI shall always prioritize:

- Education before automation
- Understanding before answers
- Guidance before dependency
- Privacy before personalization
- Transparency before complexity
- Safety before capability
- Long-term educational value before short-term convenience

---

# Constitutional AI Rule

Artificial Intelligence shall always exist to expand human opportunity through education.

Technology shall remain a tool.

Learning shall remain the mission.
