# IDENTITY CONSTITUTION

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Chapter I

## Identity Philosophy

### Article 1 — Purpose

Identity is the foundation of trust within the Freumia ecosystem.

Every verified learner, teacher, parent, school, and future institutional partner shall have a permanent digital identity.

Authentication grants access.

Identity establishes who a user is.

---

### Article 2 — Identity Before Features

Personalized educational services shall rely on verified identity rather than authentication alone.

Identity enables:

- Study Plans
- Daily Quizzes
- Leaderboards
- Certificates
- Learning Progress
- Academic Records
- Future Educational Services

---

### Article 3 — Authentication

Authentication confirms that a user can access an account.

Supported authentication methods may include:

- Email
- Google
- X
- Apple
- Phone
- Future Identity Providers

Authentication does not create constitutional identity.

---

### Article 4 — Verification

Verification establishes the educational role of a user.

Verification shall determine access to protected educational services.

Identity shall only become active after successful verification.

---

### Article 5 — Identity Roles

Freumia recognizes the following constitutional identity roles:

- Student
- Teacher
- Parent
- School
- Government
- Administrator
- Future Approved Roles

Every account belongs to one primary identity role.

---

### Article 6 — Identity Numbers

Freumia shall issue permanent identity numbers.

Examples include:

Student

FMS-ID

Teacher

FMT-ID

Parent

FMP-ID

School

FMSCH-ID

Future identity formats may be introduced while preserving backward compatibility.

---

### Article 7 — Identity Ownership

Each identity belongs permanently to its verified owner.

Identity cannot be transferred between individuals or organizations.

---

### Article 8 — Privacy

Identity information shall be protected according to the Freumia Security Constitution.

Personally identifiable information shall only be processed for legitimate educational purposes.

---

### Article 9 — Permissions

Permissions are determined by constitutional identity role.

Authentication alone shall never grant privileged educational access.

---

### Article 10 — Identity Lifecycle

Identity shall follow a managed lifecycle:

Registration

↓

Verification

↓

Activation

↓

Educational Services

↓

Graduation / Transition

↓

Historical Record

Identity history shall remain auditable where appropriate.
