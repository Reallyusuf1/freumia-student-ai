# Freumia Constitution

Version: 1.0

Status: Foundation Draft

---

## Purpose

The Freumia Constitution is the official foundational framework of the Freumia ecosystem.

It defines the philosophy, engineering standards, architecture principles, governance, security, education principles, AI principles, and long-term vision that guide every Freumia product.

This Constitution serves as the single source of truth for all future technical, educational, business, and architectural decisions.

---

## Scope

This Constitution applies to every Freumia product and service, including but not limited to:

- Freumia Labs
- Freumia Student
- Freumia Teacher
- Freumia Parent
- Freumia Schools
- Freumia Government
- Freumia APIs
- Future Freumia Products

---

## Constitutional Principles

The Freumia ecosystem shall always be built upon the following principles:

1. Education First
2. Students First
3. Freedom Through Knowledge
4. Architecture Before Features
5. AI Assists Learning
6. Privacy by Default
7. Security by Design
8. Long-Term Thinking
9. Open Innovation
10. Continuous Improvement

## Amendment Policy

The Constitution is intended to evolve responsibly.

Major constitutional principles shall only be revised after careful architectural, educational, and governance review.

No software architecture, feature, or business decision should contradict any ratified constitutional article.
